Configurable editor rulers, switched live from widgets in the same GUI. Every axis of the two heavy views is a ruler with selectable units, each drawn in its own strip beside the view, each optional per axis, and each retunable at runtime through set — which is what the menus in this window do. The time axis reads time, samples or beats (musical time on the client's grid: tempo, beatAt, quant); the waveform's amplitude axis reads norm, db, bits or percent; the spectrogram's frequency axis follows freqScale — log, linear, mel or bark. Every axis also navigates vertically: the wheel over a y-ruler strip zooms that axis around the cursor, dragging pans it, R resets. The visible window is the yStart/yLen pair, reported back as view_y, and the tick layout is adaptive — it measures its own labels, so zooming keeps revealing finer, non-colliding rungs.
