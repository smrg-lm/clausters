Adding and removing instruments

An instrument is an element, so a page can add one and take it away again. Removing it gives back everything it took — its window, the nodes its boot instantiated, and the widget, node and bus ids it drew from the page's pools — while everything else on the page keeps sounding.

Press power once (the browser wants one gesture for the whole page), then add a few instruments and remove them. Listen: the one you remove goes quiet at once and its siblings do not. Watch the counters: whatever you do, they come back to where they started.
