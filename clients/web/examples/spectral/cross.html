Two-chain spectral processing: cross-synthesis and a magnitude freeze. Where chain.html filters one chain, this def runs two FFT chains and combines them. pvMagMul scales one chain's bins by the other's magnitudes — white noise wearing the spectral envelope of a harmonic stack, which is cross-synthesis. pvMagFreeze then holds the last spectral envelope while freeze > 0: the melody stops driving the noise but the phases keep running, so the ending is the last note held as a texture. Left channel: the dry modulator, quiet, for reference. Right channel: the cross-synthesized noise. The combined chain is one def — no buffers, no buses between the stages; the spectral frames are synth-private scratch.
