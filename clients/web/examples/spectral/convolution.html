Convolution reverb: a prepared kernel through the partitioned conv UGen. The pipeline is the whole point. An impulse response goes into a buffer; buffer.gen("prepare_partconv", …) turns it into the partitioned spectra the convolver reads, sized with partconvFrames; and conv(sig, kernel, …) convolves against those ready spectra with a flat per-block cost — the partition products are spread across the hop, so a long tail does not spike the block where the FFT lands. The convolver has an intrinsic latency of fftSize / 2 samples — at 1024 that is ~10.7 ms, inaudible as the reverb's predelay here. Left channel: the dry pluck. Right channel: the convolved tail, 100% wet, so the reverb is obvious.
