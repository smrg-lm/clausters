DAW-style transport over a static timeline: play, locate, loop, position. A Pbind is a forward-only generator — you cannot seek it. A Timeline is the opposite: a static, editable list of timed items with random access by beat, so a Playhead can offer real transport controls — play({ at }), locate(beat), loop(start, end), stop() — and report a song position(). This captures a pattern into a timeline, edits it, then drives it live with the playhead. Random access happens at the boundaries; between them the playhead just scans forward.
