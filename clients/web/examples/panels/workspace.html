scroll shows its children through a window onto a virtual content area larger than the widget. It is deliberately general first: the default is the full 2D plane — drag the empty background to pan both axes, wheel to zoom anchored at the cursor — and the familiar constrained scroll views are that same widget configured down, not separate widgets: axis: "y", zoom: false is a plain vertical scroll view (the wheel scrolls), axis: "x", zoom: false a horizontal strip, and the default the free plane. All three are in one window so the degeneration is visible side by side, and the "view" x y zoom events the gestures emit appear in the log. Every pane and the reset button are named, so nothing here matches a widget id. The view state is settable back with set, which the reset button shows: its press comes in as a /gui_event and the handle answers by putting the view back at the origin and clearing the zoom (view_zoom: 0), which returns the plane to its default scale rather than pinning it to 1.
