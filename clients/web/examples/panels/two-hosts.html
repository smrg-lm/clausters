Two hosts, one page

Two independent clausters GUI hosts in one document, each driving an audio engine of its own. They share the browser tab and nothing else: their widget ids, node ids, buses and buffers are separate spaces, so both panels below are built with the same ids and neither can see the other.

A page normally wants one of each — its components belong to one mix, and guiHost() / server() give exactly that. This is the other case: several clients that happen to live in one document (isolated demos side by side, an editor beside a player) and must not share state. Each calls start() for a host and engine() for a server of its own.

host A — its own engine, playing 220 Hz

host B — its own engine, playing 330 Hz

Drag a knob and only its own tone follows. Close host A and B keeps drawing and sounding — an instance owns its canvases, its GPU surfaces, its animation tick and its audio leg, and takes them with it.
