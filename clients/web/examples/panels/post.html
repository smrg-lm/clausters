A post element holds lines of four kinds and draws each in a role of the theme: output, an error, a result (written -> value on a line of its own) and a notice (always on a line of its own). Output and errors continue the line left open.

It follows the newest line while you are at the end of it: scroll up and it stays where you read; scroll back down and it follows again. Drag to select (two clicks a word, three a line), copy with Ctrl+C, and open its menu with the secondary button. Only the rows in view are measured and drawn, so the ten thousand lines written at once cost the write.
