A scripted instrument panel: controls that round-trip values and events. It builds a view of standard controls — knobs, sliders, a number, a toggle, a button and a menu, two of them non-linear (a curved knob and a stepped number) — opens it twice, then both drives a widget live with set and listens for the events your interactions emit. No audio server is involved. The reset button of each panel carries both listening vocabularies at once, which is what puts them side by side: onEvent is the raw stream and reports its value on the press and on the release, plus the interface events beside them (press, release, and click only when the release landed on the button), while onClick fires on the completed press alone. So a cancelled press does not report less — press the button, slide the pointer off it and let go, and you still see press and release; what is missing is the clicked line, and that absence is the cancellation. The two panels are the point of the second half: a view is a definition, so opening it again gives a second instance with widget ids of its own. The same names, two independent panels.
