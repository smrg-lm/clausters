The web port of the Python client's sc_vocabulary.py: SuperCollider's vocabulary on a graph and on values -- what changed in meaning, and what is new. On the signal, the def. .pow(0.35) of a sine is a signed power, scsynth's: a bipolar signal stays bipolar and comes out squarer, where C's pow gave a NaN for every negative half. .fold(-0.6, 0.6) folds the driven tone between two bounds -- the general form of fold2 -- and an LFO moves the drive, so the folds come and go. .hanwindow() reads a ramp as a Hann window, shaping a tremolo into soft pulses; and .rand2() draws a new value every sample from the node's own seeded stream, a breath of noise that follows the envelope. On the values, the score. The degrees are a scramble of a major triad's and sixth's, turned into keys with degkey; each note's length is an exprand (equal chances per octave of duration), and its level a wchoose. All of it draws from the session's random context, so session.seed replays the same phrase on every run and in both clients.
