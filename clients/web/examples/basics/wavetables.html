The web port of the Python client's wavetables.py: the table family — buffers filled by the server itself, read as oscillators. Two wavetable-format buffers are filled with /buffer_gen sine1 (flag 7 = normalize | wavetable | clear): buffer 0 a pure sine, buffer 1 a bright harmonic stack. The wavetable flag stores the offset/slope pairs the interpolating readers expect. vosc(pos, freq) reads the table at pos and crossfades toward the next one by its fractional part, so a lagged pos gliding 0 → 1 morphs sine into saw while the note holds — which is why the two tables must be adjacent and equally sized. Buffer 2 holds a cheby transfer curve, and shaper(2, sine(freq).mul(drive)) waveshapes a pure sine through it: raising drive fades harmonics in — distortion with an exact, band-limited recipe instead of a clipped edge.
