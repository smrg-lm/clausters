The web port of the Python client's multichannel.py: a channel list, and the fold back down. dup(() => sine(freq.add(rand(-3, 3))), 12) evaluates its callable twelve times — twelve distinct sines, each with its own frozen rand detune, a thick unison bank. (dup(node, 12) would repeat one sine by reference: cheap, but twelve identical channels.) Operators broadcast over the list, so one envelope scales every channel with no loop written, and mix(bank) folds the twelve back to one signal through the server's fused sums. The last dup(sig) is the reference kind at its best: the mixed signal fanned to buses 0 and 1 — stereo out of one graph. Everything sounds through the in-page engine: no server process, no socket.
